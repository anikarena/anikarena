### Projects

**HallDesk** · residential hall management system
My university capstone, built for Mockbul Hossain Hall at City University. Seat allocation, meal planning with daily cutoffs, monthly billing, payments and complaints, with separate views for the provost, staff and students. Installs on a phone as an app (PWA) and sends push alerts. An AI assistant answers questions from the hall rules (RAG) and can take actions such as turning meals off or filing a complaint, always asking the student to confirm first. A Telegram bot sends the hall office its daily meal counts.
`Laravel` `Tailwind CSS` `Alpine.js` `SQLite` `Gemini API` `Web Push` `Telegram Bot API`

**Weather market bot** · a trading bot that showed no edge
A bot for daily temperature markets on Polymarket, using weather station forecasts, live METAR readings and a calibration step. My forecaster beat the market's own model, but a backtest with realistic order fills returned close to zero. Learning to test honestly and stop was the real result.
`Python` `Polymarket API` `METAR`
